# Publishing on itch.io

## Build the upload

    npm run itch

This writes `silicon-behemoths-itch.zip` in the project folder.

## Create the page

1. Sign in at https://itch.io and choose **Upload new project**.
2. **Kind of project:** HTML.
3. **Pricing:** No payments (free).
4. **Uploads:** add the zip and tick **This file will be played in the browser**.
5. **Embed options:** "Click to launch in fullscreen" works best. If you embed it instead, use
   1280 x 720 or larger and tick **Fullscreen button**. The game is made for a computer, not a phone,
   so leave **Mobile friendly** off.
6. **Genre:** Simulation. **Tags:** tycoon, management, business, sandbox, 3d, singleplayer.
7. itch.io has a field for it: under **Metadata > AI generation disclosure**, choose that the project
   contains AI-generated content (code, graphics and sound).

## Text for the page

**Title:** Silicon Behemoths

**Short description:** Build a tech company from a garage to a giant. Design devices in 3D, build your
own campus, factory and stores, and take on the industry.

**Description:**

Silicon Behemoths is a free tech-company tycoon game that runs in the browser.

You start as a small maker of one kind of device (phones, earbuds, watches, consoles and more) and
grow from there:

- Design every device in 3D, part by part, and watch each change show up on the model.
- Launch against a field of rival companies that react to what you do.
- Research new technology and open new lines of devices.
- Build your own campus room by room, a factory with a working production line, stores and a venue
  for launch shows you can watch.
- Hire people, form teams, sell in six regions of the world, go public, and buy out rivals.

**About this game.** This is a hobby project made for fun and given away free. All of its code, 3D
models, icons and sounds were written by an AI (Anthropic's Claude) working to one person's direction;
nothing in it was made by a studio. Every company in the game is invented. Expect rough edges, and
please say what you find.

**Controls:** mouse and keyboard. Every key can be changed under System > Keys.

**Saves** are kept in your browser. Use System > Export to file to keep a copy, or choose a sync
folder there.
